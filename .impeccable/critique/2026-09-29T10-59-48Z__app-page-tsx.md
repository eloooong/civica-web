## Overall Impression

The engineering shell is above average: honest source provenance, real deep links, a careful accessibility skeleton. What undermines it is voice and truth-telling: the homepage reads like a funded startup landing page, shows no public money, and tells users the primary feature is coming when it has shipped. Biggest opportunity: move provenance and peso-scale facts into the hero, and collapse the five defensive disclaimers into one honest status line.

## What's Working

1. Source transparency done seriously: raw endpoint URLs plus per-source docs links (page.tsx:177-207) and a footer that refuses to impersonate a government site (Footer.tsx:46-49).
2. Accessible skeleton above average, statically verified: skip link with focus reveal, global focus-visible, full landmark set, role=search + sr-only label, aria-expanded/controls/current, aria-hidden decorations, unbroken h1 to h2 to h3 order, prefers-reduced-motion honored.
3. Popular searches are real one-click deep links (page.tsx:161-167) landing in a destination that handles empty, short-query, partial-failure, and zero-result states with specific copy.
